Home Assistant / Google Nest

Authorized Google Nest cameras and thermostats connected to an owner-operated Home Assistant installation and personal home dashboard.

What this integration does

This personal integration uses Google’s Smart Device Management API and Cloud Pub/Sub for supported camera streams and events, thermostat information, and device-state updates. Available features depend on the device and the permissions approved by the owner.

It supports personal camera viewing and climate monitoring through Home Assistant and the connected Home HQ display. Thermostat information can include temperature, humidity, heating or cooling state, operating mode, and target temperatures. Supported events and media can also be used in owner-configured home automations.

Where authorized and supported, owner requests or owner-configured automations can change thermostat modes, target temperatures, and fan timers through Home Assistant. Available controls depend on the device’s capabilities and current operating state.

Owner-controlled access

The owner chooses permitted devices and features in Google’s authorization flow and can change or disconnect access through Google Partner Connections Manager.

Read this personal Nest integration’s privacy policy for data use, storage, and deletion information.

About this website

This site provides information only. It does not collect Google credentials, access Nest devices, or display their private data. This is not the official Home Assistant or Google website.
