Home HQ Calendar Sync

Google Calendar availability on a personal Home HQ frame—busy times, not meeting details.

What the app does

Home HQ Calendar Sync is a personal, owner-operated integration. Its standalone sync runs on the owner’s computer and queries one configured calendar using Google’s availability-only permission, calendar.events.freebusy.

It retrieves busy start and end times, not meeting titles, descriptions, locations, or attendees. It does not create, change, or delete calendar events.

How it works

The owner authorizes access directly with Google. The sync computer periodically transfers a busy-only snapshot to the owner’s frame, where periods appear as “Busy” with a distinct calendar label and color.

The frame displays the latest successful snapshot, rather than a continuous live connection. Updates require the sync computer and frame to be available. Once authorized and scheduled, this standalone integration does not require Codex.

Your information stays out of this website

This site provides app information and its privacy policy only. It does not connect to your calendar, collect Google credentials, or display calendar data.
